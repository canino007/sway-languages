The problem

You studied the grammar. You memorised the vocabulary. You passed the exercises.

Then someone actually speaks Spanish to you — and it all disappears.

That gap between knowing Spanish and using it is exactly what Sway is built to close. Not more rules — more speaking.

The Sway method

Four ideas, one lesson at a time.

01
Speak
01 — Speak Real conversation, from lesson one

Every lesson is built around real conversation, not rehearsed dialogue — so you get used to thinking in Spanish, not translating into it.

02 — Understand Grammar that actually makes sense

Grammar explained from the perspective of an English speaker — why Spanish works the way it does, not just a rule to memorise.

03 — Personalise Built around you, not a syllabus

Your lessons follow your level, your goals and the things you actually want to talk about — not a fixed textbook.

04 — Progress Weak points, quietly turned around

We keep track of what trips you up, so the things that were once your weak points quietly become the things you're confident in.

Meet your teacher
Portrait to be added
Álvaro

Álvaro

Spanish teacher — Linguistics & English Studies

Álvaro is a native Spanish speaker currently studying a double degree in Linguistics & Applied Languages and English Studies. That combination shapes everything about how he teaches: he's spent years looking at Spanish and English side by side, and he's particularly interested in the specific points where English speakers get stuck.

His approach is practical rather than academic. Lessons are built around speaking from the very first session, with grammar and vocabulary introduced as tools for conversation rather than subjects in themselves.

Outside of teaching, Álvaro's background in linguistics keeps him curious about how languages actually work in practice — the gap between the textbook version and the way people really speak.
