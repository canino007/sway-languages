Levels

Six levels, one clear path — A1 to C2.

Every resource in the library is tagged to a level, so you always know whether something is right for where you are now.

A1Beginner

First words, everyday phrases and the basics of the language.

A2Elementary

Simple conversations about familiar topics and routines.

B1Intermediate

Handling most everyday situations while travelling or studying.

B2Upper-Intermediate

Confident, spontaneous interaction on a wide range of topics.

C1Advanced

Flexible, effective language use for academic and professional life.

C2Proficiency

Precise, nuanced English close to a highly competent speaker.

About Sway Languages
Portrait to be added

Álvaro

Founder — Linguistics & English Studies

Sway Languages is built by Álvaro, a native Spanish speaker studying a double degree in Linguistics & Applied Languages and English Studies. That background is exactly why the library is organised the way it is: by level and by skill, the way a linguist would actually structure a language.

The goal is straightforward — a single, well-organised place to find English resources and exam preparation, instead of scattered material of uneven quality across the internet.

The library is still in its early stages and growing. New resources, levels and exam sections are added as they're ready — nothing here is presented as finished before it is.
