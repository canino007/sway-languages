Cookie policy

This page explains, in plain terms, how cookies are used on this website and how you can control them. You can change your preferences at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer.

What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit.

Necessary cookies

These keep the site working — for example, remembering your cookie preference so we don't ask again every visit. They can't be switched off, because the site won't function properly without them.

Analytics cookies

These are only set if you accept them, and help us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are most viewed. Provider: [ANALYTICS_PROVIDER]. If no analytics tool is currently active, this section is a placeholder for future use.

Booking cookies

When you open the booking widget to book a free trial, our booking provider [BOOKING_PROVIDER] may set its own cookies to manage the scheduling process. These are only loaded when you actively open the booking widget.

Managing your preferences

You can accept, reject or configure non-essential cookies at any time using the cookie banner shown on your first visit, or the "Cookie preferences" link in the site footer.
